1.Background

The Client is the developer of a boutique residential townhouse development at 30 Kiteroa Terrace, Rothesay Bay, North Shore, Auckland. One of the completed residences, Unit 2/30 (4 bedrooms, 143m² floor area, freestanding, with Code Compliance Certificate and title issued), is being marketed for sale by Roman Hu, a licensed salesperson and Head of Projects with Ray White Mission Bay (the "Listing Agent").

The Client wishes to test paid digital advertising on the Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram) as a channel for generating qualified buyer enquiries for the residence across the full buyer market (local and European families and upgraders, Korean-speaking school-zone families, and first-home and investor buyers), with the Chinese-speaking buyer segment as an additional audience the Provider is well-placed to reach — incremental to, not in place of, the mainstream market. This engagement is a fixed-budget test intended to measure channel performance before any decision on a larger project-wide campaign.

The Provider offers digital marketing and advertising services under the Magic Engine brand and agrees to deliver the scope defined in this Agreement. The Provider is not a licensed real estate agent and provides marketing services only; it does not provide real estate agency work, property advice, or investment advice.

2.Services

The Provider will deliver services described as a "Meta Lead-Generation Test Campaign" for Unit 2/30 Kiteroa Terrace during the Term.

The complete scope of Services — including deliverables, the campaign structure, managed advertising spend, quantities, and out-of-scope items — is set out in Schedule A (Scope of Services) attached to and forming part of this Agreement.

Any change to the scope defined in Schedule A must be in writing and signed by both parties (see clause 10.2).

3.Term & Timeline

This Agreement commences on the date of signing by both parties (the "Effective Date") and continues for a period of twenty-eight (28) calendar days. Advertising activity commences only after the Provider has received the fee under clause 4 and the Client-supplied assets under clause 5.

Indicative delivery milestones (subject to Client / Listing Agent turnaround):

  • Day 1–5: Setup — Business Manager, ad account, Meta Pixel and Conversions API on romanhu.com, landing page, bilingual video cuts, WhatsApp intake, campaign build
  • Day 6–12: Lead-generation campaign live; English / Chinese ad variants A/B tested; viewing-booking flow and email newsletter active
  • Day 13–25: Optimisation — scale the winning creative / language; weekly reporting
  • Day 26–28: Final performance report and data package handover

Content review and asset-approval turnaround is expected within two (2) business days of each request. Delays in Client or Listing Agent feedback may extend the Term proportionally.

11.Campaign Data, Leads & Privacy

11.1 Enquiry delivery

Buyer enquiries generated by the campaign arrive as messages in the inbox of the Listing Agent's own Facebook Page (Roman Hu Real Estate) and are answered by the Listing Agent directly. The Client acknowledges that the development is co-listed by multiple agencies and that enquiries generated under this campaign are directed to the Listing Agent.

11.2 Advertising audiences — ownership

The audiences built during the campaign (people who watched the video creative, engaged with the Page, or started a conversation) are derived from engagement with the Listing Agent's own Facebook Page. Accordingly:

(a) during the Term, those audiences are used solely to market Unit 2/30;
(b) after the Term, they remain a continuing asset of the Listing Agent as the Client's nominated channel, and the Client may reach that same audience again through the Listing Agent on future projects — each subsequent project therefore starts from a warmer base;
(c) the Provider does not treat those audiences as its own asset, and will not use them to promote a competing agent or a competing development.

The Provider retains only its own operating infrastructure (Business Manager, advertising account, pixel / dataset) and aggregated, non-personal performance insights and methodology.

11.3 Privacy

Both parties will comply with the Privacy Act 2020. Personal information (names and contact details) is provided voluntarily by buyers within their own messages to the Listing Agent and is held by the Listing Agent. The Provider does not upload any personal contact information to Meta or to any other third party, and builds audiences only from anonymous, platform-held engagement signals. The Provider acts as a data processor on behalf of the Client and the Listing Agent for any individual enquiry data it handles.

11.4 Hand-back on termination

On termination or expiry the Provider will, within 10 working days and at no charge, provide the Listing Agent with: (a) the full definition of each audience built — its source, rule and retention window — in a form that allows it to be recreated in the Listing Agent's own advertising account; (b) the audience ledger showing how each audience grew over the Term; and (c) any individual buyer contact details captured by the Provider. The parties acknowledge that the underlying engagement data resides on the Listing Agent's own Facebook Page and is not withheld by the Provider.
