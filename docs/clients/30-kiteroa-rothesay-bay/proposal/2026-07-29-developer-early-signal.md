## The signal worth your attention

One enquiry that came through the ad opened with interest in **Lot 7**, was given price guidance, and then asked:

> *"And what about unit 2–5?"*

Another asked for the address and floor plan before anything else.

**These are people shopping the development, not a single address.** They found it through an ad for one unit and immediately widened the question to the others.

That matters for two reasons:

1. **Demand is not unit-specific.** Buyers reaching this project are comparing lots against each other. Marketing one unit surfaces buyers for all of them.
2. **Those enquiries are currently landing on one agent's desk for one listing.** Every buyer who asks "what about the other lots?" is a buyer the project already paid to reach.

---

## What is running

- Five creative variations racing against each other (drone, interior, school-zone, price, and a trilingual version) — the platform automatically shifts budget to whichever performs best
- Targeted at Auckland's North Shore, within 15km of Rothesay Bay
- Anchored on the Rangitoto College in-zone position, which is the strongest single hook in this catchment
- Every enquiry answered personally, in the buyer's own language where needed (English / Chinese / Korean)

---

## Honest read at this stage

- **It is early.** Three days and NZ$62 is a signal, not a proven cost-per-sale. The cost per enquiry has risen as the local audience is covered repeatedly (it started lower); this is normal and expected in a small geographic catchment.
- **An enquiry is not a buyer.** Some will be neighbours or curious locals. What we can say is that several have asked for floor plans, price guidance and viewing times — which is buying behaviour, not browsing.
- **We are not claiming a sale.** We are reporting what the money bought: real conversations with people actively comparing lots in this development.

---

## The question this raises

The campaign was scoped to one unit. The enquiries are not.

If the same engine were pointed at the development as a whole, every one of those "what about the other lots?" questions would already have somewhere to go.
