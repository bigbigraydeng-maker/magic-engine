Can I combine goods from different suppliers?

Yes. East Inter offers warehousing and consolidation in Guangzhou and Shenzhen. Contact our team before dispatching goods to confirm the receiving address, storage terms and packing requirements.

Should I choose sea or air freight?

Sea freight generally suits larger shipments when your schedule is flexible. Air freight suits urgent and higher-value goods. Share your cargo details and required arrival date so our team can recommend an option and confirm current transit estimates.

What is included in my freight quote?

Your tailored quote should set out the agreed freight, collection, clearance and delivery charges. Ask our team to confirm taxes, duties, insurance and any destination charges for your particular shipment before booking.

What do I need to request a quote?

Provide your origin, destination, goods description, number of packages, total weight and outer dimensions. For multiple package sizes, include a packing list.

How can I check my shipment’s progress?

Use the shipment update tool above to prepare an email with your East Inter reference. The freight team can provide the latest update; live tracking is not connected to this website yet.
