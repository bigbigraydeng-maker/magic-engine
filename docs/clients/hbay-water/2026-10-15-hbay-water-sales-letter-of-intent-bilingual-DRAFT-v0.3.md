## 1. PURPOSE / 目的

1.1 Party A wishes to sell HBay Water products in New Zealand and overseas markets. Party B is willing to help by contacting and negotiating with potential sales channels on Party A's behalf. This Letter records the parties' shared intention and the way they will work together while a definitive commission arrangement is prepared.
甲方希望在新西兰本地及海外市场销售 HBay Water 产品。乙方愿意代表甲方接触潜在销售渠道并进行洽谈。本意向书记录双方的共同意向，以及在正式分成约定拟定期间的合作方式。

1.2 Except for Clauses 6 to 11 (which are binding), this Letter is a statement of intention only and does not oblige either party to enter into a sales arrangement or to complete any sale.
除第 6 至 11 条（具有约束力）外，本意向书仅为意向声明，不要求任何一方必须订立销售安排或完成任何交易。

## 2. MARKETS AND CHANNELS / 市场与渠道

2.1 **Territory / 服务区域:** New Zealand (local), Singapore, Taiwan and Hong Kong. Other markets may be added by written agreement. / 新西兰本地、新加坡、台湾和香港。其他市场经书面同意可增加。

## 3. WHAT PARTY B WILL DO / 乙方的工作

3.1 Within the markets in Clause 2 and with Party A's agreement, Party B will: / 在第 2 条所列市场内，经甲方同意，乙方将：
- (a) research and shortlist potential channel partners; / 调研并筛选潜在渠道伙伴；
- (b) make first contact and introduce HBay Water, using materials Party A has supplied or approved; / 使用甲方提供或批准的材料首次接触并介绍 HBay Water；
- (c) hold exploratory discussions, collect each channel's requirements (pricing, volumes, labelling, certifications, logistics) and report them to Party A; / 开展初步洽谈，收集各渠道的要求（价格、数量、标签、认证、物流）并向甲方汇报；
- (d) support Party A in negotiations, including preparing proposals and follow-up. / 协助甲方洽谈，包括准备方案与跟进。

4.1 **Party A is the seller.** Party A decides whether to sell to any channel, sets prices and terms, signs the sale contracts, invoices, ships and collects payment. / **甲方是卖方。** 由甲方决定是否向某渠道销售，设定价格和条款，签订销售合同，开票、发货并收款。

Party A supplies samples, product information and price lists on request. / 甲方对产品及关于产品的所有宣称负责，并负责各市场的出口与进口要求（含许可、认证、标签、食品安全和报关）。甲方应按要求提供样品、产品资料和价格表。
