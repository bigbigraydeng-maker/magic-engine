About English videos

The lip-sync model is trained mainly on Mandarin, so mouth movement is less precise in English. English videos therefore lean on real footage and captions, with the presenter smaller on screen. We check every frame before a video goes to you.

More real footage, better videos

The top half of each video uses real footage of your warehouse, stock and job sites. Phone photos and 10–20 second vertical clips sent by WhatsApp are all we need.

Rules

  • Only a presenter who has signed consent; the digital twin can’t be changed to another person without re-recording
  • Every video is checked for text, prices, logo and lip-sync before it goes to you for approval
  • Videos are labelled as AI-generated where platforms require it; only prices and product facts you confirm are used
