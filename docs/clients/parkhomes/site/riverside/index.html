A masterplanned community on Park Estate Road

Riverside is Park Homes’ largest development — a masterplanned residential community of 107 homes on Park Estate Road, Rosehill, arranged along a landscaped river corridor.

Released in stages, it brings together two- to five-bedroom homes at a range of sizes and price points — considered living, close to established amenity and transport in Auckland’s south.
