## 4. TARGET AND CONTROLS / 目标与控制措施

4.1 **Target, not a guarantee.** Party B works to a target ROI between 1.15 and 1.50 and shall use reasonable endeavours to sell as many tickets as practicable within the Ad Budget. This is a target for planning and review. It is not a promise of any ROI or number of tickets, or that Party A will not make a loss, and 1.50 is not a cap on sales. / **目标，非担保。**乙方以 ROI 1.15 至 1.50 为目标，并尽合理努力在广告预算内尽可能多地售出门票。这是用于计划和复盘的目标，不是对任何 ROI、票数或甲方不亏损的承诺，1.50 也不是销量上限。

4.2 **How ROI is measured.** ROI = Ticket Revenue ÷ Total Outlay (NZD 5,000.00). Ticket Revenue = the number of paid tickets sold with the codes ME25 and IN25 × NZD 13.50. The only data source is the "Promotion Codes" report in Party A's Eventbrite account; figures reported by Meta or any other advertising platform are not used. Free tickets (including free entry for children under 16) are excluded. / **ROI 的计算。**ROI = 票款收入 ÷ 总付出（NZD 5,000.00）。票款收入 = 使用折扣码 ME25 和 IN25 售出的付费门票张数 × NZD 13.50。唯一数据来源是甲方 Eventbrite 账户中的"折扣码"报表，Meta 或其他广告平台自报的数字不予采用。免费票（包括 16 岁以下免费入场）不计入。

4.3 **Controls.** At the reviews on 8 and 10 November 2026, Party B reports spend, Ticket Revenue and cumulative ROI by ad set. Cumulative ROI means Ticket Revenue to date divided by the part of the Total Outlay that matches the ad spend to date (the Total Outlay × ad spend to date ÷ the Ad Budget). If it is below 1.0, Party B halves the daily budgets of all ME Campaigns from the next day and reports this to Party A in writing that day. If the ad cost per paid ticket of an ad set (its spend ÷ paid tickets sold through its tracking link and a code) is above NZD 9.98 on two consecutive days, Party B pauses that ad set. Within the Ad Budget, Party B may move budget between ME ad sets without fresh approval. / **控制措施。**在 2026 年 11 月 8 日和 10 日的复盘中，乙方按广告组报告花费、票款收入和累计 ROI。累计 ROI 指累计票款收入除以与累计花费相对应的那部分总付出（总付出 × 累计广告花费 ÷ 广告预算）。如低于 1.0，乙方自次日起将全部 ME 广告的日预算减半，并于当天书面报告甲方。如某广告组的每张付费票广告成本（其花费 ÷ 通过其追踪链接及折扣码售出的付费票数）连续两天高于 NZD 9.98，乙方暂停该广告组。在广告预算范围内，乙方可在 ME 广告组之间调配预算，无需另行批准。

4.4 By 17 November 2026 Party B delivers a one-page close-out reconciliation (codes, tracking links, spend by ad set, Ticket Revenue and final ROI). It is for information and does not change any amount payable. / 乙方应在 2026 年 11 月 17 日前提交一页结案对账（折扣码、追踪链接、各广告组花费、票款收入和最终 ROI），仅供告知，不改变任何应付金额。
