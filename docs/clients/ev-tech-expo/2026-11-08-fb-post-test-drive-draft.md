Thinking about going electric? Take one for a spin first. ⚡🚗

At the NZ EV & Tech Expo you can test drive EVs on a purpose-built route right next to Auckland Showgrounds — about 15–20 minutes per vehicle, and you can line up drives back to back.

Best part: test drives are included with your general entry ticket. Just show your ticket confirmation at the gate.

Good to know before you come:
✅ Full NZ driver's licence required (learner and restricted licences can't drive)
✅ Book your test drive time online — slots are limited and go first come, first served

📅 13–15 November 2026
📍 Auckland Showgrounds
🕚 Fri 11am–6pm · Sat & Sun 10am–5:30pm

Grab your ticket and book your drive — link in the comments 👇
