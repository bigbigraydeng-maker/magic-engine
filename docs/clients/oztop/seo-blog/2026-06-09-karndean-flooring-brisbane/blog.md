## Karndean for Brisbane climate: what to choose

Brisbane's humidity, summer storm season, and harsh north-facing sun all influence which Karndean colour and finish wears best long-term. A few practical observations from installing Karndean across hundreds of Queensland homes:

**For north-facing rooms with strong sun exposure**, the mid-tone and darker variants (Embered Blackbutt, French Grey Oak, Sterling Oak) hide UV-related colour shift better than the very pale oaks. Karndean's UV-stable wear layer reduces fading, but pale colours under decade-long direct sun will always show some lightening.

**For wet areas, laundries, and pet zones**, LooseLay Longboard's 100% waterproof construction is a real advantage over engineered timber. The product handles spills, dog accidents, and tracked-in rain without lifting or warping — provided the perimeter trim seals the edge against splash zones.

**For rental and investment properties**, we generally recommend the harder-wearing oak tones (Wheat Oak, Field Oak, Tasmanian Oak) — they hide tenant wear-and-tear scratches better than the high-contrast designer colours and re-let well between tenancies.

**For high-end new builds**, the designer greys and Pure series tend to hold their visual appeal as decor trends shift, where a strong yellow-toned oak might date faster.

If you're not sure which range suits your specific room, bring photos and your floor plan to the showroom — our team has installed every colour in the LooseLay Longboard range and can tell you what works.
