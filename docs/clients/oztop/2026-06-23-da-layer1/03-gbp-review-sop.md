# Google Business Profile 评论征集 SOP

> **重要性**：GBP 评论数 = 本地 SEO 排名因子 **#1**（比 backlinks 还重要）
> **目标**：6 月达 25+ 评论 / 12 月达 50+ / 平均 4.5⭐+ / 每月新增 5-10 条
> **谁主导**：PM 决策 + FDE 日常执行

---

## 🎯 评论 = 本地 SEO 杠杆 #1

Google 本地搜索算法权重（公开估算）:
- **30%** — GBP 评论数 + 平均星级
- **25%** — Local citation NAP 一致性（见 § Citation SOP）
- **20%** — On-site SEO（已做 ✅）
- **15%** — Backlinks（DA Roadmap Layer 3）
- **10%** — User behavior signals

**结论**：拿 50 条 4.5⭐ 评论 = 比 12 月内容产出还重要。

## 📋 5 个评论征集渠道（按转化率排）

### 🥇 渠道 1：付款时实体店二维码（转化率 30-50%）

**做法**：
1. 印一个 **A5 桌牌** 放收银台 / 打印台
2. 桌牌内容：
   ```
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
   Help other Brisbane homeowners
   Scan to leave a Google review
   [QR code to GBP review link]
   
   Thank you! 
   The Oztop Team
   ```
3. 客户付款时 staff 主动指 — "If you're happy with what you got today, can you scan and leave us a quick review? Takes 30 seconds."

**QR 生成**：
1. GBP dashboard → **Get more reviews** → 拿 review URL（短链格式 `https://g.page/r/xxxx/review`）
2. 用 https://www.qr-code-generator.com/ 生成 free QR code → 贴到 A5 模板

**效果**：付款时心情好（拿到货）→ 转化率最高的瞬间。

---

### 🥈 渠道 2：安装完成后 SMS 提醒（转化率 15-25%）

**做法**：安装完成后 **第 3 天**（不是当天 — 让客户实际用 3 天看效果）发 SMS:

```
Hi {Name}, this is Oztop. How are you finding your new flooring/tile?

If you're happy, would you share a quick Google review? It really helps 
other Brisbane homeowners decide.

[review link]

Reply STOP to opt out. — Mark @ Oztop
```

**重点**:
- 不要每个客户都发（只发安装完成的，不发到店询价的）
- 个人化 — 加客户名 + staff 名字
- "STOP to opt out" — 符合 AU SPAM Act

**效果**：客户已经在用 → 真实体验 → 评论更有说服力

---

### 🥉 渠道 3：Email follow-up（转化率 10-15%）

**做法**：安装完成后 **第 7 天** 发 email:

```
Subject: How's your new flooring, {Name}?

Hi {Name},

It's been a week since we installed your new flooring at {Address}.

How is it looking and feeling?

If you're enjoying it, we'd love a quick Google review — it helps other 
Brisbane homeowners find us. Takes 30 seconds:
[Big button: Leave a Review]

If anything's not 100% right, hit reply — I read every email personally.

Cheers,
{Staff Name}
Oztop Building Supplies
```

**效果**：低成本，可批量。

---

### 渠道 4：实体店免费小礼物（转化率 50%+，但限量）

**做法**：客户在店里付款后，staff 说：
> "We've got a small thank-you — a 10mm SPC sample block. If you can leave us a Google review now, it's yours."

把 10mm SPC 样品（库存里几乎免费）当小礼物。客户在店里立刻评论 → 转化率最高。

**注意**：Google 政策禁止"用礼物换好评"，但 "感谢 review 的客户" 不算违规（措辞重要）。

---

### 渠道 5：WhatsApp business（转化率 20-30%）

**做法**：FDE 跟客户日常沟通（送货安排、安装确认）的最后一条消息：
> "All sorted. If you're happy with how it turned out, would mean a lot if you left us a Google review: [link]. Cheers!"

---

## ⚠️ 必做 vs 不做

| ✅ 必做 | ❌ 不做 |
|---|---|
| 真实客户邀请评论 | 假评论 / 自己写 |
| 评论 = 一种 "感谢" 不是 "买卖" | 用现金/折扣换好评（Google 政策违反） |
| **24 小时内回复每条评论**（哪怕 5 星）| 不回评论（信号:不在乎客户）|
| 评论里如有问题 → 公开道歉 + 私聊解决 | 删除负面评论（Google 不让删, 反而会推到顶）|

## 📊 评论回复模板

### 5 星好评回复（24h 内）

```
Thanks {Name}! We're really glad the new {flooring/tile/carpet} is working 
out for you. If you ever need anything else for your Brisbane home, 
you know where to find us. — The Oztop Team
```

### 4 星评论（24h 内）

```
Thanks {Name}, we appreciate the honest review. We're always looking to 
improve — what would have made it 5 stars for you? Feel free to email 
us at info@oztopbuildingsupplies.com.au.
```

### 3 星或以下评论（**1 小时内**回复）

```
Hi {Name}, I'm really sorry to hear about your experience with {issue}. 
This isn't the standard we want our Brisbane customers to have. Can we 
talk through it? Please email me directly at info@oztopbuildingsupplies.com.au 
or call (07) {phone} and I'll make it right.

— {Owner Name}, Oztop Building Supplies
```

**关键**：负面评论 + 公开诚恳回复 = 比 50 条好评更说服潜在客户。
