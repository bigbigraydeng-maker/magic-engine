# CTS Spotlight SEO Checklist — 3 October Tours + Silk Road 2027

> Date: 2026-06-03
> Goal: turn the `Spotlight` / October campaign hub into the main SEO entry point for CTS' three 2026 China departures, while keeping `Silk Road 2027` as the high-end planning lane.

## Working assumption

- The core campaign URL is `ctstours.co.nz/campaigns/october-2026`.
- If that page already exists, we upgrade it.
- If it does not exist yet, we create the spotlight hub first, then point all October campaign traffic there.
- The three 2026 departures are equal peers inside the hub:
  - `Beijing & Xi'an`
  - `Shanghai & Surroundings`
  - `Chongqing & Chengdu`
- `Silk Road 2027` sits beside them as the planning / premium future lane, not as a hard-sell departure.

## Primary business signals

- Three 2026 departures:
  - `Beijing & Xi'an` — `Oct 15` — `NZD $3,480`
  - `Shanghai & Surroundings` — `Oct 14` — `NZD $3,399`
  - `Chongqing & Chengdu` — `Nov 1` — `NZD $2,999`
- Planning lane:
  - `Silk Road 2027` — `NZD $7,999`
- Audience: `New Zealand travellers`
- Strongest objections to handle:
  - Visa-free entry for NZ passport holders
  - Seat scarcity in late July / early August
  - Which of the three departures fits which traveller type
  - Why Silk Road should be a planning enquiry, not an impulse buy

## Keyword cluster to test first

- `best china tour from nz`
- `china tours from new zealand`
- `beijing and xian tour nz`
- `shanghai and surroundings tour nz`
- `chongqing and chengdu tour nz`

## Checklist

- [ ] Confirm the spotlight page URL and whether it already exists in WordPress or the public site.
- [ ] Decide whether the hub is a single page with four cards or a hub page plus four child pages.
- [ ] Align the page title, meta description, and H1 around `Best China Tours from NZ` or a similar hub phrase, then place the four trip names under it.
- [ ] Put the most important decision signals above the fold: the three departure dates, the three prices, the seat scarcity message, visa-free entry, and Auckland departure.
- [ ] Add a short summary block for each of the three 2026 tours, so users can quickly self-sort.
- [ ] Add a separate `Silk Road 2027` planning block with calm, premium language.
- [ ] Add FAQ answers for the top objections: visa rules, seasonality, which tour suits which traveller, and why Silk Road is planning-only.
- [ ] Add internal links from related CTS content to the spotlight page.
- [ ] Add or verify `Tour` / `FAQPage` schema, canonical, Open Graph, and sitemap inclusion.
- [ ] Create one supporting article only if the spotlight page is ready: `Which CTS China tour suits you best?`
- [ ] Refresh older campaign links so they point back to the spotlight hub instead of fragmenting traffic.
- [ ] Check that the CTA is consistent across the page and campaign assets.

## Publish order

1. Spotlight hub / campaign landing page
2. Supporting article, if needed
3. Existing campaign assets updated to point at the hub
4. Final QA: title, meta, canonicals, links, schema, sitemap

## Done when

- The spotlight page is the single clearest entry point for the three 2026 departures.
- `Silk Road 2027` is present as the planning lane, not hidden in footer copy.
- The page has a clear search-intent keyword target.
- Campaign assets and support content all point back to the same hub.
- We can hand this to the CTS team without explaining the SEO logic again.
