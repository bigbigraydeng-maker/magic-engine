## POST 方案（共3条）

> 核心逻辑：Post 被搜索索引，第一句话必须包含核心关键词，写得有料让人愿意展开看完。

---

### Post 1 — 移除最大顾虑（周二发布）

**第一句（关键词必须在此）：**
> *NZ passport holders: You don't need a visa to enter China right now.*

**正文：**
> China's visa-free policy for New Zealand passport holders covers tourist stays up to 30 days — and it's confirmed through 31 December 2026.
>
> Our October Beijing & Xi'an tour departs well within that window. No visa application, no consulate appointment, no extra cost.
>
> Just your passport, your bags, and 10 days across two of the world's great cities.
>
> Forbidden City. Great Wall at sunrise. High-speed rail to Xi'an. Terracotta Warriors. Muslim Quarter lamb skewers.
>
> CTS Tours has been running China tours from New Zealand since 1928. We handle everything — flights guidance, internal transfers, licensed local guides, and same-day support if anything comes up.
>
> October departures from Auckland. From NZD $3,480 per person.

**结尾提问（促评论）：**
> Had the visa thing been stopping you? You might have just run out of excuses 😄

---

### Post 2 — 为什么两个城市都要去（周四发布）

**第一句：**
> *Beijing or Xi'an — most people think it's one or the other. It doesn't have to be.*

**正文：**
> The two cities are 1,200km apart but connected by one of the world's fastest rail networks. We take the bullet train between them — 4.5 hours, through the heart of China, with views that make the journey part of the trip.
>
> In Beijing: Forbidden City, hutong evenings with local families, Mutianyu Great Wall (the quieter section, not the tourist circus).
>
> In Xi'an: the Terracotta Warriors — "the scale is genuinely hard to believe until you're standing in front of it" — plus the Muslim Quarter's lamb skewers and pomegranate juice.
>
> 10 days. Both cities. One itinerary.
>
> —
> October 2026 departure | NZD $3,480 | Visa-free for NZ passports | Limited seats

**结尾提问：**
> Which would you prioritise — Beijing or Xi'an? (We're biased: you really do need both.)

---

### Post 3 — 建立紧迫感（周日发布）

**第一句：**
> *October is China's best travel season from NZ — and that's exactly why our tours fill up fast.*

**正文：**
> The weather is ideal. The crowds are manageable. And the visa-free window is open.

>
> Beijing & Xi'an. 10 days. From NZD $3,480. NZ passport? No visa.
>
> If you're curious, the easiest thing to do is message us or click the link below. No commitment, just information.

**结尾：**
> 🔗 See the full October itinerary → [link]

## STORY 方案（每日1-2条，整周滚动）

> 核心逻辑：Story 受众已认识 CTS，目标是推动从"感兴趣"变成"发了消息"。直接、有行动指令、制造适当紧迫感。

| 天 | Story 内容 | 行动按钮 |
|---|---|---|
| 周一 | 客人真实评价卡片："10 days, two ancient capitals, zero complaints" | 查看行程 |
| 周二 | 免签提示图卡："NZ Passport ✓ No Visa Needed ✓ October 2026" | 了解详情 |
| 周三 | 长城日出图 + "想看这个画面？" | DM 我们 |
| 周四 | 行程5大亮点快闪卡（一条Story，5张图） | 预订咨询 |
| 周五 | 互动问答："你去过中国吗？" 投票贴纸 Yes / Not yet | — |
| 周六 | 席位提醒："October seats filling — last year sold out in July" | 立即咨询 |
| 周日 | 价格 + 日期 + "明天开始新一周，今天先问问" | 联系我们 |

**Story 设计原则：**
- 每条必须有数字或截止日期
- 不要温柔的 Story，每条都要有行动指令
- 隔天放一张完工/旅游实景图（无滤镜，社会证明）
