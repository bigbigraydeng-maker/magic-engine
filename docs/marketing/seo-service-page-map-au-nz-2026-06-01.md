# AU/NZ Service Page Map for Magic Engine

Generated: 2026-06-01

## Positioning Guardrail

Magic Engine should speak to **digital marketing SMEs, founders, and lean growth teams** in Australia and New Zealand.

We can still capture agency-shaped searches as discovery keywords, but the public surface should **not** position the business as a traditional large full-service agency.

That means:

- Lead with practical outcomes, not retainers
- Lead with SME-scale budgets, not enterprise programs
- Lead with training, visibility, and execution support, not broad agency positioning
- Keep bilingual support visible without making the site feel like a generic multilingual agency template
