About Magic Engine

Who we are

Magic Engine is an AI upgrade, GEO, and training company built for ambitious Australian and New Zealand businesses — especially for founders and operators who know their product is good but want a clearer public surface in Google, AI assistants, social feeds, and review sites.

We started Magic Engine on a simple observation: most businesses have no idea how they actually appear online. They've never seen the version of themselves that lives in search results and AI answers — but their customers and competitors have. We use free diagnosis, GEO, and training to close that gap.

Our name

GEO helps your homepage, service pages, training pages, and contact flow stay readable for search engines and answer engines. The goal is not more pages — it is clearer signal.

What we do

We help businesses do three things:

  • Diagnose it — A free discovery report reveals how you show up across search, AI search, social, ads, reputation and against your competitors.
  • Fix it — Magic Engine turns each finding into real work — content, website fixes, bilingual updates, training tasks and ad decisions — that you approve and we execute.
  • Prove it — You watch your visibility improve over time inside one simple portal.

Who we serve

We work with small and growing businesses across Australia and New Zealand, including Chinese-speaking and English-speaking teams that want one public surface to support both audiences.
