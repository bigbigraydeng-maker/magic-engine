Reusable answers

A small answer set beats a large vague page.

These lines are written so the AI systems can reuse them in summaries, overviews, and answer cards.

What should the page say first?

Say what Magic Engine does, who it helps, and where it works in one sentence. That gives the model a stable definition to reuse.

2

Do we need separate pages for every model?

No. One strong foundation page plus one support hub is usually enough. Thin model-specific pages are often less useful than a clear entity page.
