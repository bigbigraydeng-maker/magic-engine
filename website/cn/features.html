Platform features

广告表现：诊断、报告和修复。

Magic Engine connects your advertising accounts, surfaces what's working and what's wasting spend, and applies optimisations — only ever with your explicit approval.

Connect your ad accounts

Securely connect your Google Ads and Meta accounts. Magic Engine reads your campaign data using the official platform APIs — no screen-scraping, no shared passwords, no third-party credential stores.

Google Ads

Connect via Google OAuth. Magic Engine reads campaign, ad group, keyword and performance data from your account using the Google Ads API. Your account credentials never leave Google's authorisation flow.

Meta (Facebook & Instagram)

Connect via Meta Business authorisation. Magic Engine reads your campaign performance, audience data and ad creative health across Facebook and Instagram ad accounts.

Reporting & diagnosis

Magic Engine pulls your campaign, ad group and performance data into clear reports and a health diagnosis — so you see exactly what's working and what's wasting spend.

Campaign health report

A plain-English diagnosis of your account: which campaigns are profitable, which ad groups are underperforming, and where budget is being wasted on low-intent clicks.

Performance trends

Week-over-week and month-over-month tracking of your key metrics — cost per click, conversion rate, ROAS — with automatic flagging of significant changes.

AU/NZ benchmarking

Performance benchmarks calibrated for Australian and New Zealand markets. Know whether your click costs are typical for your industry and region, not a US average.

EOFY opportunity window

Automatic alerts before the AU EOFY (30 June) and NZ EOFY (31 March) windows. Know when to increase spend to capture seasonal intent at the right time.

Campaign management

From the diagnosis, Magic Engine recommends optimisations and generates ad creative. With your approval, changes are applied across platforms.

AI-generated optimisations

Magic Engine analyses your diagnosis and generates specific, actionable recommendations: bid adjustments, negative keyword additions, ad copy improvements, and budget reallocation suggestions.

Approval-first execution

Every recommended change is presented for your review before anything is applied. You see exactly what will change, what it's expected to do, and what the rollback looks like. Nothing goes live without your sign-off.

Ad creative generation

Generate new ad copy, headlines and descriptions tailored to your AU/NZ audience and current campaign goals. Review and approve before publishing.

Cross-platform coordination

Run consistent campaigns across Google Ads and Meta simultaneously. Magic Engine coordinates messaging, budget and timing so your channels work together rather than competing.

How we use the Google Ads API We use the Google Ads API to read your campaign, ad group and performance data for reporting and diagnostics, and — only with your explicit approval — to apply optimisation changes such as bid adjustments, keyword updates and ad copy modifications to your account. We do not make changes without your review and confirmation.
