How we work

Facts first. Marketing implications second.

Magic Insight is Magic Engine's research publishing brand and a member benefit—not a seventh product pillar, a separate platform or a custom-report service.

Start with the publisher

Figures are taken from published primary or clearly identified authoritative sources. Period, scope and source type travel with the number.

Separate evidence from judgement

What the source states, what we calculate and what Magic Insight concludes are presented as different layers.

Leave the gaps visible

If a missing dataset could change the recommendation, the gap belongs in the brief rather than being filled with an assumption.

What we study

The conditions that make a market move work—or fail.

Market demand

Where demand is growing, which measurement is relevant and which headline hides the real movement.

Policy and logistics

The rules, thresholds and supply constraints that change the commercial model.

Channel behaviour

How search, social, reputation, AI visibility and local networks shape demand in each market.

Competitive movement

Which alternatives are winning attention and what their position reveals about the market.
