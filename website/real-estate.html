Frequently asked questions

Clear boundaries before implementation.

Is the same system used for agents and developers?

They share the Magic Engine platform, but the workflows are different. Agents focus on reusable audience growth; developers focus on sustained brand and project demand.

Does Magic Engine replace our real estate CRM?

No. It can operate as a growth and action layer around an existing CRM, advertising account and website.

Who owns the audience and lead data?

The client retains its own audiences, named contacts and interaction records. Raw client data is never transferred between clients.
