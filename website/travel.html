Lead management

The lead is not the result. The result is a managed path to conversion.

Travel enquiries decay when product context is lost or follow-up is slow. Magic Engine keeps intent, source and next action connected.

Capture product and source context

Know which destination, itinerary, content or campaign created the enquiry.

Resolve named contacts in CRM

Merge real enquiry activity into the appropriate contact record without pretending anonymous visitors are identifiable people.

Prioritise the next action

Learn from actual outcomes

Frequently asked questions

What the travel solution does—and does not do.

Is this only an SEO service?

No. SEO and GEO are acquisition capabilities inside a wider system connecting content, campaigns, enquiry capture, CRM follow-up and conversion learning.

Can Magic Engine work with our existing travel website and CRM?

Yes. We improve the current journey and connect existing tools where practical instead of automatically requiring a rebuild.

How does content become a lead?

Content is mapped to traveller questions and products, distributed across relevant channels, connected to clear enquiry paths and tracked into CRM follow-up.

Does Magic Engine guarantee bookings?

No. It improves the measurable path from discovery to enquiry and follow-up. Booking outcomes still depend on product fit, pricing, availability and sales execution.
