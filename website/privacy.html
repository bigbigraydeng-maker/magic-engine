Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Magic Engine AI Technology Limited ("Magic Engine", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website and services, including the free 张骞 Discovery report. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and, where applicable, the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: the website URL you submit, your name and email address, and (optionally) your business name, goals, and a competitor URL. If you apply for the Magic Engine waitlist, we also collect your company or brand, current country or market, industry, intended target market, relevant marketing platforms, approximate monthly marketing budget, website, and any market-entry context you choose to provide.
  • Information we gather to build your report: publicly available information about your business — such as your search rankings, AI-search mentions, public social profiles, public advertising signals, and public reviews. The public-source Discovery report does not access private accounts. Optional account connections are described below; we do not require your social-account passwords.
  • Technical information: basic analytics such as pages visited and general device/browser type, collected to operate and improve the site.

Free website check. When you use the free website check, we store the homepage address you enter (or the three answers you give if you have no website) and the findings, so we can email you the full report if you ask for it. To stop abuse of the free checks, we record a one-way code derived from your network address (never the address itself) and keep it for at most 2 days.

1.1 Optional TikTok account connections

If you choose to connect a TikTok account, TikTok asks you to authorize the permissions requested by Magic Engine. We receive the authorized account identifier, basic profile information, granted permissions, and authorization credentials. Credentials are stored encrypted on our servers and are used to maintain the connection; we do not receive your TikTok password.

When you explicitly confirm a post, we send TikTok the video, caption, visibility, interaction, and commercial-content settings you select. TikTok receives a hosted video link to retrieve the approved file. We store the publishing task identifier and processing outcome so you can check the result. Connecting an account does not itself authorize a post.

You can revoke Magic Engine's permissions in TikTok. Revocation stops authorized account operations but does not delete posts already on TikTok. You may request deletion of stored connection information or other personal information by emailing hello@magicengine.cloud. Connection information and publishing records are retained only as needed to provide the connected service and meet applicable obligations.

2. How we use your information

  • To generate and deliver your 张骞 Discovery report.
  • To assess your waitlist application, understand demand for our early workflows, and contact you about early access or a relevant managed partnership.
  • To email you about your report, your account, and your use of Magic Engine.
  • To provide, maintain, and improve our services.
  • To comply with our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. How we share information

We share information only with service providers that help us run Magic Engine, under appropriate confidentiality terms, including:

  • Email delivery (to send your report and account emails).
  • Hosting and infrastructure (to run our website and platform).
  • Analytics (to understand and improve site usage).
  • Connected platforms: when you expressly approve a TikTok post, we transmit the selected video and post settings to TikTok to carry out that request.

We may also disclose information where required by law.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to operate the site and may use analytics cookies to understand usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings. By default we keep cookie use to the minimum needed to run the service.

5. Data storage and security

We retain the information you provide and your report results only as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal requirements, after which we delete or de-identify it. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. Information may be stored or processed in Australia and other countries where our service providers operate.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you, and you may unsubscribe from our emails at any time. To make a request, email hello@magicengine.cloud. If you have a concern about how we handle your information, you may also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) or, if you are in New Zealand, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (OPC).

7. Children

Magic Engine is intended for businesses and is not directed to anyone under 18.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll post the updated version here with a new effective date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@magicengine.cloud.
