FAQ

The questions AI search tends to reuse.

Do I need a separate page for every model?

No. One strong foundation page can support several models as long as the structure, answer blocks, and local signals are clear.

Does this work in English and Chinese?

Yes. We want the same business entity to be readable in both languages, so the site feels consistent instead of split.

Should we start with the homepage or service pages?

Usually the homepage, the GEO page, and the main service pages are enough to create a strong first signal.
